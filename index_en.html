Be Happier

自由と公正のもと、
一人ひとりがより幸福に
生きようとする機会を
創り続けられる世界

The future is not something to be predicted. It is something to be created. Bringing together the wisdom and passion of 52 individuals, this report charts a path toward a Purposeful Future. Looking ahead to the society we aspire to realize by 2050, it presents a roadmap for shaping the future through shared values, guiding principles, and innovative business models.
